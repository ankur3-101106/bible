## Full Description
Sanctuary is a distraction-free, offline-first Bible planner and reader designed for daily Scripture engagement and spiritual formation.

### Key Features
* **Dynamic Planning Engine**: Smart daily chapter calculations that balance your reading workload evenly across your target dates.
* **Dynamic Redistribution**: Missed reading days? Easily recalculate your cadence to catch up without losing your progress history.
* **Offline KJV Bible Reader**: Read all 1,189 chapters of the King James Version offline with customizable fonts, reader themes, bookmarks, notes, and verse highlights.
* **Daily Reminders**: Non-redundant background reminders via WorkManager.
* **100% Private & Local**: Zero ads, zero tracking, and no internet connection required.
